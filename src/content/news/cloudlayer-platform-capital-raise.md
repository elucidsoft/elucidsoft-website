Today, Elucidsoft announces a major evolution in the product direction of **cloudlayer.io**, expanding from its foundational document generation API into a unified, HTML-first visual content, document, and form platform. To support this growth and scale our global infrastructure, Elucidsoft is formally opening a capital raise for cloudlayer.io.

## The new platform direction

Since its initial launch in 2020, cloudlayer.io has operated as high-performance rendering infrastructure, translating HTML, web URLs, and dynamic templates into pixel-perfect PDFs and raster images via REST API. 

Modern operations, marketing, and engineering teams, however, routinely wrestle with fragmented tooling: visual form builders that lack robust PDF generation, document templating tools that cannot handle dynamic code, and screenshot capture services isolated from business workflows.

cloudlayer.io is addressing this divide by consolidating these capabilities into a single structured artifact platform:

- **Unified visual & code authoring**: Design complex layouts using a drag-and-drop visual editor or author directly in semantic HTML/CSS with Nunjucks templating, with full round-trip fidelity between visual and code modes.
- **Comprehensive rendering & capture**: High-throughput generation for PDFs, raster graphics, full-page screenshots, and dynamic media assets.
- **Hosted forms & workflow automation**: Embeddable forms, e-signature workflows, and webhook-driven batch processing integrated directly with document rendering pipelines.
- **Multi-language SDK ecosystem**: Official client libraries for JavaScript/TypeScript, Python, PHP, .NET, Go, Java, Ruby, and Rust.

## Opening capital raise

To accelerate platform feature parity across visual document creation, enterprise form management, and automated capture workflows, Elucidsoft is actively raising seed capital for cloudlayer.io.

Investment proceeds will directly fund:

1. **Global rendering cluster expansion**: Scaling multi-region compute infrastructure to minimize conversion latency and increase batch rendering throughput worldwide.
2. **Enterprise workflow features**: Deepening visual form logic, multi-party e-signatures, team collaboration spaces, and compliance audit tooling.
3. **Developer & partner ecosystem**: Broadening native integrations with cloud storage providers, workflow engines, and enterprise software suites.
